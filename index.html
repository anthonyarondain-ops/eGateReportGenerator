How H.A.Z.E. analyzes

Transparent, offline rule-based matching. H.A.Z.E. suggests hypotheses; the technician verifies the actual root cause.

01 · Symptom matchingMatches words and component context against the offline knowledge base.
02 · Ranked possibilitiesShows likely causes, supporting clues, and confidence as a match score.
03 · Safe next checksPrioritizes non-invasive checks and escalates safety or hardware faults.
Match score = symptom keyword overlap, not a failure probability. Always verify findings on site.
